 Analysis of Your Invoice Generator for Document Understanding Model
Your invoice generator script is a solid foundation for training a document classification and information extraction model. Here's my assessment:

Strengths of Your Current Approach
Diverse data generation: Creates varied invoices across different sectors (retail, tech, DIY)
Layout variations: Multiple layouts, fonts, colors, and styles
Format options: Outputs in PDF, JPEG, and JPG formats
Realistic content: Includes genuine-looking company info, invoice numbers, dates, and line items
Data augmentation: Implements image transformations (rotation, brightness, contrast, noise)

2. Pipeline Improvements
Document preprocessing: Add OCR integration (Tesseract or AWS Textract)
Field normalization: Standardize formats for extracted numbers and dates
Training data: Consider these additions to your generator:
Add more defects (coffee stains, folds, stamps)
Include more invoice layouts from additional sectors
Add handwritten annotations to some invoices
Generate some non-invoice documents for negative samples
3. Evaluation Strategy
Create a dedicated test set with annotated bounding boxes and text for each field you want to extract (total_ht, total_ttc, date, client_name, etc.)

Overall Assessment
Your generator creates excellent training data for this task. For a complete solution, you'll need:

An OCR component to extract text and positions
A document classifier using LayoutLM
A NER component to identify fields in recognized invoices
A post-processing system to normalize and store extracted data
Consider using LayoutLMv2/v3 if you want to leverage visual features alongside text and layout information.
